How we work

Direct access. Clear responsibility.

There is no account-manager layer and no hand-off after the first conversation. You talk to John, he does the work, and when extra capacity is needed he brings in people he already trusts. That is the whole model.

  • One consultant accountable for the engagement
  • Contractors added only when the work calls for it
  • Fixed-scope assessments and pilots, agreed up front
  • Monthly software maintenance retainers for ongoing support
