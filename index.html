FAQ

Straight answers.

Is our data safe if we use AI?

Yes — that's designed in from day one. We use business-grade AI services that don't train on your data, keep sensitive information inside your environment where required, and can use privately hosted models when the situation calls for it.

Do we need to replace our old systems first?

No. Most of our work connects AI and automation to the systems you already have. When modernization does make sense, we do it incrementally so the business never stops running.

We're not sure where AI fits. Is that a problem?

Not at all — that's what the assessment is for. Bring the tasks that are slow, repetitive, or error-prone and we'll tell you honestly where AI helps and where a simpler fix is better.

How is pricing structured?

Assessments and pilots are fixed-scope with a fixed price agreed up front. Ongoing maintenance and support is a monthly retainer sized to your needs.

Can you support an application someone else built?

Absolutely. Inheriting undocumented Access, VB.NET, and C# applications is a big part of what we do. We start by reviewing and documenting it, then fix what's urgent.
